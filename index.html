Every Greek
One Friend Away From Jesus

For the past 20 years, we have witnessed the love of Jesus breaking through in Greek life at San Diego State University. Fraternities and sororities are some of the most influential communities on college campuses, but they are often places where faith feels out of place. Greek IV at SDSU provides a space for students in Greek life to explore their faith and ask questions about Jesus.

Through honest conversations, deep community, and the true love of God, students are discovering that faith and Greek Life don’t have to be separate—and that following Jesus can transform their lives, their chapters, and their campus.

We believe that every Greek can be one friend away from Jesus.

Today's Students

Something is stirring in the hearts of God's people across the globe, and that same stirring is active in SDSU Greeks. This generation of students is marked by a rich intimacy with Jesus and a deep commitment to bringing their friends to His feet. The word "revival" naturally flows out of their mouth as if it's something normal and familiar to them. It is a gift to see decades of prayers answered through these students, and we're excited for you to hear some of their stories at our upcoming reunion!
